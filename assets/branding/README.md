# VillaCam icon

Original artwork generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, transparent PNG: `villacam-source.png`. Prepared launcher preview: `villacam-icon-preview.png`.

Android packaging is reproducible with `tools/build-launcher-icons.ps1` (ImageMagick). It crops to the meaningful alpha bounds without repainting the artwork, preserves the alpha channel of the foreground and generates adaptive layers plus legacy square/round icons at mdpi through xxxhdpi. The original generated PNG is retained unchanged.

Final ImageGen prompt:

Create one polished Android launcher icon foreground for an educational live camera broadcasting app called VillaCam. No text or letters. A bold compact white rounded video camera silhouette, with a bright emerald/turquoise lens and three simple wireless broadcast arcs integrated at its upper right. Flat graphic design with subtle dimensional polish, high contrast, friendly professional school television identity. Transparent background, no tile, no border, no mockup, no shadows extending far. Square canvas. Entire camera plus arcs contained in central 60% of canvas with generous equal transparent padding on every side, for safe circular Android adaptive icon cropping. Crisp strong shapes readable at 48 pixels. White and emerald #34D399 palette, designed to sit on solid dark navy #111827.
